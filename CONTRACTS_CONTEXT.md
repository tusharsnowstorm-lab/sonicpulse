# SONIC PULSE — CONTRACTS CONTEXT

Master brief for the contracts session, which owns **every agreement the
festival signs**: vendors, artists, venue, media, volunteers, stall-holders
and the published attendee terms. Written 13 Aug 2026 — **the event is 43
days out**.

Companions: `VENUE_CONTRACT_CONTEXT.md` (venue deep-dive — site
requirements, load-in, permits, commercial terms; read it before drafting
anything venue-related), `EVENT_CONTEXT.md` (physical event),
`FINANCE_CONTEXT.md` (money, KNOWN vs OPEN), `OPS_CONTEXT.md` (timeline and
workstream status).

Currency BDT (৳); the owner sometimes writes 1,00,000 for 100,000.
**Never invent a figure, date, or party name marked OPEN** — raise it as a
question. Statuses are as of 13 Aug 2026; verify anything that may have moved.

  narcotics or illegal substances anywhere on the premises.

## 3. Contract 1 — installation vendor (the one with agreed terms)

**Scope.** Six of the nine art installations — **COILGATE** (entrance
serpent arch), **GLOWTIDE** (jellyfish walkway), **MYCELIA** (five large
overhead mushroom canopies), **EMBERHART** (monumental stag), **CHROMA**
(climbable beast with lit staircase), **THE EMPTY THRONE** (climbable
chair with twin flame torches) — **plus the Main Stage: seven pillars,
tallest approximately 40 ft, built to an agreed blueprint** (the blueprint
is the controlling document and should be attached).

Not in this vendor's scope: the other three Echoes — **EVENT HORIZON**
(bridge-of-light dressing), **CLOUD NINE** (nine guest-loaded net bays),
**ICARUS** (the kite burned at midnight) — plus activities, general site
lighting, and the second stage.

**Commercials (labour only — the owner buys all materials).**

| Line | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Artist fee, six installations | ৳220,000 |
| Main Stage build | ৳100,000 |
| Additional workers, installations | ৳60,000 |
| Additional workers, Main Stage | **OPEN — agree in writing before stage work begins** |
| Materials, both scopes | **Owner-borne, unestimated** — vendor owes itemised specs in advance |

**Delivery.** All six installations complete and delivered by **20
September 2026**. Timely delivery and professional quality are **material
terms**, with termination and cure/rectification rights; per-day late
penalties were recommended given a single-night event with zero slack.
Structural safety sign-off is tied to quality — a failed safety inspection
counts as non-delivery.

**Exclusivity.** The vendor is bound as the organiser's **exclusive art-
installation provider until 31 December 2028** — no installation work for
competing events or competitors. In return the organiser commits to
**engaging them with new work every 4 months**; if no work is provided
within **6 months following the festival (i.e. by ~25 March 2027)**, the
exclusivity lapses and the vendor is free. Drafting notes already flagged:
define "competing events/competitors" concretely, state the reciprocal
work commitment as the consideration supporting the restraint, and expect
Bangladeshi courts to read multi-year restraints narrowly.

**Two live issues to resolve in this contract:**

1. **"Delivered by 20 Sep" is now ambiguous.** The owner confirmed (13 Aug)
   that **installations are fabricated off site and transported to the
   venue on festival day (25 Sep)**. So the 20 Sep milestone sensibly
   means *fabrication complete and inspected at the workshop*, with
   transport and erection on 25 Sep. Say that explicitly, and add who
   bears transport, damage in transit, and on-site erection labour.
2. **Main Stage assembly timing is unconfirmed.** Whether a 40 ft,
   seven-pillar stage is also day-of assembly or needs earlier on-site
   days has not been established with the vendor. It determines the venue
   access window — ask before either this or the venue contract is signed.

## 5. Contracts not yet started — what each must cover

- **Light & sound, and power.** Two stages across 17.5 continuous hours,
  UV rigs, plus power and distribution to nine lit installations;
  generators, redundancy and overnight fuel. Continuous-operation
  obligations and failure remedies matter more than usual — there is no
  second day to recover a dead stage.
- **Ground security.** ID checks at the gate (all ages since 13 Aug 2026),
  overnight coverage, marshals for two climbable structures, the ember
  line and burn perimeter at midnight, lake and pool watch. Enforcement of
  the alcohol-free and no-harassment policies is theirs; **licensed bar
  supervision is not** (there is no bar).
- **Housekeeping / sanitation.** Toilets for 800+ across 17.5 hours,
  overnight servicing, waste, drinking-water points, morning-after
  clearance including **ash and debris from the Great Burn**.
- **Artists.** Six named headliners — **Fly on the Wall, Izhaqo,
  Vampbetch, Psytaraa, Drip, Rii** — with set times already published.
  Fees, riders, travel/hospitality, cancellation and recording/likeness
  rights are all OPEN. Published set times create an expectation; booking
  confirmations should be papered promptly.
- **First Pulse winners (×2).** Open-call artists taking the 4:00–7:00 PM
  opening slots. Whether they are paid, or receive tickets and hospitality
  only, is OPEN. Their applications carry personal data — see §7.
- **Videography and photography.** A quotation is under evaluation. Any
  media contract must address: the **no-live-streaming policy** (§6), the
  ban on attendee professional cameras (official crews work on organiser
  credentials), **copyright and licence scope** (who owns the aftermovie
  and for how long), deliverable formats and deadlines, one-take moments
  (the midnight Burn, sunrise), overnight crew shifts, and **drone use —
  permission not secured**.
- **Food vendors and bazaar stall-holders.** Pitch fees or revenue share
  (OPEN), hygiene and licensing, open-flame cooking safety, waste, hours
  ("the quarter never closes while the music plays"), and consistency with
  the published bans on outside food and drink, glass bottles, and alcohol.
- **Activity operators.** Styx boats (passenger safety, capacity 2–4 per
  boat, night operation), Neon Lagoon pool (lifeguard cover, dawn swim),
  Ember Rites fire troupe (performer insurance, fire safety), Starside
  guide and equipment, Warpaint UV artists and skin-safe supplies.
- **Volunteer programme.** Graduating students acting as guest-facing
  guides, unpaid, receiving **certificates of service** for university
  applications. Draft as a volunteer agreement, not employment: no wage,
  no employment relationship, defined shifts and duties, code of conduct,
  liability waiver, and what the certificate attests. Settled 13 Aug 2026:
  **50 Wayfinders**, **two 8-hour shifts** (Dusk 3:00 PM–11:00 PM, Dawn
  11:00 PM–7:00 AM), certificates issued by **Dhaka Music Festival alone**,
  open to **final-year undergraduates and HSC/A-level finishers**. The
  programme name **WAYFINDER** is locked. **Live issue:** "graduating
  students" may include under-18s, and while the event itself is now all
  ages, volunteers work an unpaid overnight shift in a guest-facing role —
  a **volunteer minimum age, and guardian consent if minors are accepted,
  is an OPEN owner decision**. The application form collects date of birth
  so any rule can be applied.
- **Creator / media-pass agreements.** Currently informal barter — a free
  ticket in exchange for coverage. Paper it lightly but clearly: what
  coverage is expected (posts, stories, tagging @sonicpulsefestival),
  usage rights letting the festival repost, the no-live-streaming rule,
  conduct, and that the pass is non-transferable. Note the acquisition
  channel is shifting to inbound applications after an Instagram
  restriction on the organiser account (auto-expires 2 Sep 2026).
- **Insurance.** Public liability for 800+ overnight guests, with specific
  cover for fire (performers, torches, the Burn), working at height and
  two climbable public structures, water activities, and the 40 ft stage.
  Whether the venue requires being named as additional insured is OPEN.

## 6. Published attendee terms — already binding, do not contradict

These are live on sonicpulsefestival.com and function as the consumer-
facing contract. Every vendor agreement must be consistent with them:

- **Tickets non-refundable**; all sales final. **If the event is cancelled
  for reasons outside the organiser's control, tickets carry over to the
  next edition** rather than being refunded — a deferred liability, and a
  reason to negotiate vendor and venue force-majeure clauses hard so a
  cancellation does not leave the organiser paying twice.
- **Tickets non-transferable**; one ticket, one entry; name must match the
  NID presented at the gate.
- **All ages** — the strict 18+ rule was removed on 13 Aug 2026. ID verification (NID, passport or birth certificate) is still enforced at the gate. A guardian/accompaniment policy for minors is an OPEN owner decision that affects security scope, venue permit and insurance.
- **Prohibited**: professional cameras/recording equipment, outside food
  and drinks, alcohol, narcotics and illegal substances, weapons, glass
  bottles, personal speakers.
- **No live streaming** to any platform during the event; personal photos
  and video are fine.
- **Safe space** — harassment, bullying or discrimination of any kind is
  not tolerated; violations mean removal and possible permanent ban.
- Commitments the venue and vendors must physically support:
  **wheelchair-accessible pathways and viewing areas at both stages**, a
  **quiet/chill-out zone**, **covered areas** (the event proceeds in light
  rain), **limited on-site parking plus a marked drop-off/pick-up zone**,
  and a **site map** emailed to ticket holders.
- **Venue address is confidential** until announced to ticket holders by
  email — reflect this in venue and vendor publicity clauses.

## 7. Legal environment and standing cautions

- **Governing law: Bangladesh.** Draft for enforceability there, not for
  US/UK templates. Multi-year restraints (the vendor exclusivity through
  2028) are read narrowly as restraint of trade and need genuine
  consideration — which the every-4-months work commitment supplies.
- **Personal data.** The festival collects NIDs for ticket verification and
  publishes a data-protection promise: stored in private encrypted cloud
  storage, never publicly accessible, access limited to authorised staff
  and logged, with **Bangladesh Digital Security Act obligations**
  referenced. Any contractor touching attendee data (security, gate staff,
  ticketing, admin) must be bound to match that standard. First Pulse and
  volunteer applications carry personal data too.
- **Image and likeness.** Attendees are filmed and photographed; artists
  are recorded; creators post. Media contracts need clear rights grants,
  and attendee-facing terms should cover filming on site.
- **High-risk activities** — fire, height, water — should carry explicit
  risk allocation, indemnities, and proof-of-insurance obligations rather
  than generic boilerplate.
- **Single-night event.** Any clause assuming a multi-day hire, a "day 2",
  or a rescheduling window is an error.

## 8. Standing clause checklist for every Sonic Pulse contract

Correct contracting party (see §1) · scope with an attached specification
or blueprint · fees in BDT with a payment schedule tied to milestones ·
who supplies materials · delivery/performance dates that reconcile with
25 Sep and the 20 Sep fabrication milestone · quality standard with
cure rights · termination for late or substandard delivery · safety
compliance and sign-off · insurance and indemnity · force majeure aligned
with the ticket carry-over policy · confidentiality of the venue address ·
image/media rights · alcohol-free and no-live-streaming site rules ·
compliance with the published attendee terms · governing law Bangladesh ·
dispute resolution.

## 9. Working rules for this session

- Never invent a number, date, or party detail marked OPEN — list it as a
  question for the owner.
- Flag conflicts between contracts rather than resolving them silently:
  the venue access window, the installation delivery definition, and the
  Main Stage assembly timing are currently interdependent and unsettled.
- When a term changes money, tell the owner so the **finance session** can
  record it; when it changes dates or vendor status, so the **planner
  session** can reschedule; when it changes something published on the
  site, so the **website session** can amend it.
- Keep this file current: when a contract is signed, when a counterparty
  is appointed, or when an OPEN item is decided, say so — the owner
  maintains it in the project repository.
