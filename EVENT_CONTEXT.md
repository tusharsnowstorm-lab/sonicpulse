# SONIC PULSE — PHYSICAL EVENT CONTEXT

A self-contained brief on the physical event, written to drop into any new
session (vendor quotes, production planning, logistics). Everything here is
sourced from the festival's locked plan and published site
(sonicpulsefestival.com). Names in CAPS lockups are locked — never rename,
translate or paraphrase them.

## The event in one paragraph

Sonic Pulse is Bangladesh's first sunset-to-sunrise music festival, organised
by Dhaka Music Festival (@dhakamusicfestival; festival account
@sonicpulsefestival). One single night: Friday 25 September 2026, gates 4:00
PM, running continuously to 9:30 AM Saturday — 17.5 hours. Dhaka; exact venue
TBA (announced to ticket holders). Outdoor grounds with a grove of trees, an
open field, a lake with a short bridge, and a pool deck. Capacity 800+,
**open to all ages** (the strict 18+ rule was removed 13 Aug 2026),
ID-verified entry — NID, passport or birth certificate. Audience is Gen Z / millennial,
underground-electronic-music-centred.

## Scale at a glance

- 17.5 hours continuous, overnight, one night only — no second chance at any shot
- 2 stages running **in sequence** (never overlapping): Main Stage 4:00 PM – 4:30 AM, Sunrise Stage 4:30 – 9:30 AM
- 10 programme blocks (8 artist sets + 2 ritual blocks)
- 12 large-scale themed art installations ("The Twelve Echoes") along a trail from gate to ritual ground
- 9 named activities running through the night, several of them visual set-pieces
- 800+ attendees, all ages, single entrance through an installation arch
- Approx. 14 hours of the event are in darkness or low light

## Stages

- **Main Stage** (4:00 PM – 4:30 AM): the big build — seven pillars, tallest
  ~40 ft, full production lighting and sound, UV rigs. Peak-time techno and
  house.
- **Sunrise Stage** (4:30 – 9:30 AM): intimate, melodic, deliberately faces
  east so the sunrise happens behind/over it. Softest light of the event and
  its signature closing imagery.

## The Twelve Echoes (installation trail, gate → ritual ground)

All monumental scale (12 ft+ unless noted), all internally lit, all placed to
be walked as a trail:

1. **COILGATE · The First Loop** — entrance arch: a coiled serpent guests walk through; ember-lit scales, teal glass eye. Every guest's first and last frame.
2. **GLOWTIDE · The Migration of Dreams** — avenue of glowing jellyfish suspended overhead along the main walkway.
3. **SWAYBLOOM · The Four Swings** — four bamboo arch swings wrapped in fresh flower wreaths beside the Glowtide walkway.
4. **EVENT HORIZON · Portal of Light** — the lake bridge dressed as a tunnel of light; boats pass beneath it.
5. **SHOAL · The School of Light** — a bamboo pyramid of hanging translucent fish above a water bowl on the lake shore; light falls through them and floods the ground in moving colour.
6. **MYCELIA · Dream of the Forest Floor** — five giant lit mushroom-medusa canopies hanging in the grove.
7. **OVERSTORY · The Woven Crossing** — a hand-woven walkable net laced through the grove canopy above Mycelia.
8. **EMBERHART · Keeper of the Wilds** — monumental antlered stag over the open field, ember-lit eyes.
9. **CHROMA · Beast of Broken Light** — climbable iridescent beast; lit staircase up the mane; a horn that throws a light beam.
10. **THE EMPTY THRONE · Seat of No King** — climbable oversized chair, twin flame torches; the grounds' highest guest vantage point.
11. **CLOUD NINE · The Star Nets** — nine raised bamboo-framed net bays guests lie in; edge-lit; doubles as an activity.
12. **ICARUS · The Last Transmission** — monumental bamboo-and-woven-light kite at the ritual ground; collects written ribbons all night, then is **ceremonially burned at midnight (THE GREAT BURN)**. Exists only until midnight — a one-take event.

## Activities (running through the night)

- **EMBER RITES · Dancers of Flame** — fire performance waves at the ritual ground; final rite leads the crowd to the Great Burn.
- **THE GREAT BURN · Night of Release** — midnight: field goes dark, drums stop, Icarus burns, a minute of silence, then the heaviest drop of the night. The single most important moment of the event.
- **WARPAINT · Skin of Light** — UV body-paint station; paint blazes under stage UV rigs.
- **NEON LAGOON · Water That Glows Back** — pool deck, water lit cyan-to-magenta; the 7 AM swim during the sunrise sets is a rite.
- **STARSIDE · The Whisper Hour** — telescope stargazing on the dark side of the grounds (deliberately unlit zone), 4:30–6:30 AM.
- **STYX · The Silent Ferry** — lit boats (2–4 guests) drifting a lantern-lined lake channel, passing under Event Horizon.
- **BAZAAR OF ECHOES · Trades of Wonder** — night market in the temple lanes; 3 AM barter hour.
- **FEAST QUARTER · Fire and Ice** — open-flame cooking lane opposite a cold side (iced chai, kulfi); never closes while music plays.
- **CLOUD NINE · The Star Nets** — see Echoes XI; best 4–6 AM.

## Light conditions across the night

- 4:00 – 6:30 PM: daylight into golden hour
- ~6:30 – 7:00 PM: sunset (during Fly on the Wall)
- 7:00 PM – 5:45 AM: full darkness — installation glow, stage production light, fire (Ember Rites, torches, the Burn), UV; the Starside zone is deliberately near-dark
- ~5:45 – 6:30 AM: dawn/sunrise over the lake and Sunrise Stage
- 6:30 – 9:30 AM: morning light

## Rules that affect cameras and coverage

- **No live streaming from the venue by anyone during the event** (published site policy — applies to attendees; any official live coverage would need an explicit owner decision to carve out).
- Attendees may take personal photos/video, but **professional camera equipment is prohibited for attendees** — official crews operate on credentials/media passes issued by the organiser (a creator/media-pass programme already exists).
- Venue is outdoor, September in Dhaka: heat, high humidity, chance of light rain (event proceeds in light rain), dust, and smoke/embers near the ritual ground at midnight.
- Drone use: no permission secured yet; Dhaka airspace rules and venue permission are unresolved — treat as an open item any vendor must answer for, not an assumption.
- Venue address is TBA — site recces must be scheduled once the venue locks.

## One-take moments (missable, non-repeatable)

1. THE GREAT BURN at midnight — Icarus destroyed by design; includes a dark-field silence before the drop (hardest exposure conditions of the night immediately followed by fire)
2. Sunset during the dusk set (~6:30–7 PM Friday)
3. Sunrise behind the Sunrise Stage during Drip (~5:45–6:30 AM)
4. The 3 AM bazaar barter hour
5. First guests entering through COILGATE at gates-open
6. The 7 AM Neon Lagoon swim during the sunrise sets

## What full coverage actually spans (scale implications)

Any production quote should be read against this reality: two stage locations
plus a twelve-installation trail, a lake (boats), a pool, a market lane and a
ritual ground; 17.5 continuous hours of which ~14 are night; fire, water, UV
and smoke environments; a hard one-take set-piece at midnight and a second at
sunrise — meaning crew shifts (no single operator can cover 4 PM to 9:30 AM
alert), multiple simultaneous positions during the Burn, genuine low-light
capability, weather/dust protection, on-site data backup across the night,
and explicit deliverables for both long-form (aftermovie) and short-form
(social reels for a Gen Z/millennial audience) output. Anything in a quote
that assumes a "one-day event" daylight shoot with a single crew does not
match this event.
