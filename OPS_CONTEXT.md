  site plan → decor + installation placement → recces for media crews.
- Vendor material specs → owner purchases → fabrication start → 20 Sep
  delivery. Chase the specs list first; purchasing lead time is inside the
  vendor's deadline.

  emails. Every week sales stay closed compresses revenue before 25 Sep.

  programme push (which itself waits on the 2 Sep Instagram unlock or paid
  promotion).
